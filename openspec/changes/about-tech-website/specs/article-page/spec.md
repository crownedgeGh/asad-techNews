## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Article View
The system SHALL provide a dedicated page for reading individual articles.

#### Scenario: User opens an article
- **WHEN** a user navigates to an article URL
- **THEN** the page displays the Title, Author, Date & Time, Media, and formatted Content.

### Requirement: Social Sharing
The article page SHALL allow users to share the article.

#### Scenario: User clicks share
- **WHEN** a user clicks the share button
- **THEN** a copy link option and social media app icons are displayed.

### Requirement: User Engagement
The article page SHALL display related content and a comments section.

#### Scenario: Reaching the end of an article
- **WHEN** a user scrolls past the main content
- **THEN** they see a Comment Section and a Related Articles list.
